## Design direction (per Saiful's request: human, premium, not "AI-generic")

- Warm editorial "print" look: ivory paper background, warm ink text, deep
  bottle green (Narayanganj river green) as the single brand hue, clay accent
  reserved for future status badges.
- Fraunces (optical sizing) for display type; Geist for UI and mono for
  metadata/counts. Hairline borders instead of shadows; restrained radii.
- No gradients, no glassmorphism, no default indigo/purple.
